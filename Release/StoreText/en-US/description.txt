Your books have stories to tell. Listen with Folisona, a free, ad-free reader that gives your own library a voice.

FELL ASLEEP WHILE LISTENING?
Folisona remembers when each passage began playing and shows small time labels beside the text. When you wake up, check your listening times, find the last passage you remember and tap to pick up the story. You can also browse earlier listening sessions. These timestamps help you find your place; Folisona does not detect when you fall asleep.

YOUR LIBRARY, READ ALOUD
Import DRM-free EPUB books, text-based PDFs, TXT and Markdown files. Listen to installed Apple voices in Spanish and English while following the words on screen. Download more voices in Settings > Accessibility.

ONE CHAPTER AFTER ANOTHER
Browse chapters in order, choose where to start and prepare audio for a chapter or a whole book. PDFs are divided using their outline or pages.

TAKE THE STORY WITH YOU
Prepared audio keeps playing with your screen locked or while you use other apps. Folisona remembers your reading position and reuses saved audio.

SPEECH GENERATED ON YOUR DEVICE
Your text is processed locally to create narration. Audio preparation can take time; a progress indicator keeps you informed.

Free. No ads. Just your books and time to listen.

Folisona does not include books, translate text or recognise text in scanned documents. Prepare audio with the app open before listening in the background.
