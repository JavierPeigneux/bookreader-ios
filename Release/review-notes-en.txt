Folisona is a free, ad-free reader for user-imported DRM-free EPUB, text-based PDF, TXT and Markdown files. No sign-in is required.

Speech is generated locally using the bundled Kokoro model. Please test narration on a physical iPhone/iPad. Select a chapter and prepare its audio with the app in the foreground. Prepared audio supports background playback.

Listening time labels show when a text block started playing. Use the clock menu in the reader to show timestamps and browse previous sessions. Tap a timestamp to return to that passage. This is a reading-history feature, not sleep detection.

Store screenshots use an isolated fictional library and listening history to demonstrate the real interface. This fixture is compiled only in DEBUG, not the distribution build.
